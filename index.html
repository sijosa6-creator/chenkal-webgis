About Chenkal

Geographical and administrative profile

A landscape in southern Kerala

Chenkal Grama Panchayat is located in Neyyattinkara Taluk of Thiruvananthapuram district, Kerala. It forms part of Parassala Block Panchayat.

Its landscape includes settlement areas, agricultural land, roads, water bodies and the surrounding rural environment. Geographic information can support local planning, infrastructure development and environmental management.

  • District: Thiruvananthapuram
  • Taluk: Neyyattinkara
  • Block Panchayat: Parassala
  • Village: Chenkal
  • Area: 19.37 sq. km
  • Western boundary: Neyyar River
  • Northern boundary: NH 47 / NH 66 corridor
  • Southern neighbours: Kulathoor and Karode
  • Southeastern neighbour: Parassala

Administrative statistics are based on the panchayat's official website and should be checked for future updates.
